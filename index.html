cMod

Chronological Model Ontology Suite

Persistente Identifikatoren für die Ontologien OCP, OCCP, OULD und AMO. Die Ontologie-Dateien selbst werden mit Abschluss der Dissertation veröffentlicht.

Persistent identifiers for the OCP, OCCP, OULD and AMO ontologies. The ontology files will be released with the completed dissertation.

Status. Identifiers are reserved via w3id.org. RDF downloads and HTML documentation are not yet served at these IRIs.

IRIs

https://w3id.org/cmod          suite
https://w3id.org/cmod/ocp     Ontology for Chronological Processes
https://w3id.org/cmod/occp    Ontology for Chronological Construction Processes
https://w3id.org/cmod/ould    Ontology for Updates and Linked Data
https://w3id.org/cmod/amo     Area Model Ontology

Term namespaces use a trailing hash, e.g. https://w3id.org/cmod/ocp#.
